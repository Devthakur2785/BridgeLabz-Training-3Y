The Pink City

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its beautiful palaces, forts, colorful markets, rich history, and traditional culture.

Famous Places to Visit

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a magnificent historic fort known for its architecture, courtyards, and beautiful views of the hills.

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most recognizable landmarks. Its many windows were designed to allow cool air to flow through the building.

City Palace

The City Palace is a grand complex containing courtyards, museums, royal buildings, and impressive Rajasthani artwork.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur has a vibrant cultural heritage. Traditional handicrafts, folk music, dance, festivals, colorful clothing, and local artwork are important parts of the city's identity. The city combines royal history with modern life.

Traditional Rajasthani Food

Jaipur is also famous for its delicious Rajasthani cuisine. Popular dishes include Dal Baati Churma, Gatte ki Sabzi, Ker Sangri, and traditional sweets. Local markets and restaurants offer many opportunities to experience authentic flavors.
